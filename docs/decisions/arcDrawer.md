# Arc drawer: looped sections, compact or expanded switcher, colour-picker fan (wave 26)

Source: owner batch of wave 26, clauses 1 to 3. References are from a drawing app (Concepts): its quick-switcher quarter ring (marked 1) and its expanded colour picker.

## Decisions
- **Sections loop.** The corner drawer's sections (A-Z letters, categories, or recent buckets) form a ring. `ArcMath.wrap/step` is the one rule: past the last continues into the first and back; one section stays; none is harmless. Swipe or drag along the arc, scroll wheel, Left/Right (`PuckKeys.step` in core-input, which no longer clamps), and the TalkBack custom actions "Next section" and "Previous section" all use it. The quarter-ring switcher shows the selected section and the next ones with wrap, so the first section is drawn again after the last.
- **Switcher style setting.** "Switcher: Compact or Expanded" (Ground Control, home and drawer group, `LauncherPrefs.switcherStyle`, pref key `switcherStyle` in `launcher_prefs`, so it is exported with the other prefs). Default Expanded. Only the two options exist. Compact draws each wedge with its icon only. Expanded draws the icon (or letter) plus a small number (the section's app count), the selected wedge dark with a coloured edge arc on the ring, a round hub at the corner holding the Radar glyph.
- **Expanded drawer = colour picker.** Opening the selected wedge fans the quarter ring out into concentric rings of rotated square cells (52 dp, 6 dp gap), one ring per section stacked outward from the hub (the section's header cell first, then its apps: icon in a rounded light cell with a small label running radially). The hub shows the mode row (A-Z / Category / Recent) and the Radar glyph (the search glyph), and the ring actions. Dragging along an arc turns that ring, with fling inertia; tap launches; long press opens the app menu.
- **Polar layout is pure math** (`ArcMath`, `ArcDrawerMathTest`): angles are measured from the corner's own edge, so the other corner is the same numbers with x mirrored. Ring k sits at `hubOuter + gap + cell/2 + k*(cell+gap)`; the cell pitch is derived from the radius so neighbours never touch; the u window keeps whole rotated cells on screen in portrait and landscape; a ring that cannot show one cell is dropped. Text is rotated so it never reads upside down (turned the other way on right-hand corners).
- **Cheap draw.** Cells are positioned in the layer/offset phase from state read lazily; the tracks, hub and wedges are one Canvas with no per-frame allocation (strokes, metrics and the scrim are remembered).
- **Accessibility.** Wedges are 48 dp tabs ("Name, N apps", selected state, expand/collapse and next/previous actions); cells carry the app label and `collectionItemInfo`, section headers are headings; hardware keyboard: arrows, Tab, letters, Enter, Esc (existing puck contract). Reduced motion: geometry is final at once and only an alpha fades.
- **Removed:** the old WheelDrawer spoke and tangent-text wheel and its geometry helpers (`WheelGeometry` keeps the pure parts still used: normalisation, angular velocity, letter buckets, grouping).

## Not done / device-only
- Nothing here was run on a device; the screenshots are Robolectric renders. Fling feel and the real 52 dp touch ergonomics are device-only.
- The drawer is dark in both themes (a black scrim and cells in light ink), as the wave 25 Radar is; light and dark qualifiers render the same palette.
