# App drawer: alphabet wheel, app fan and up to four shortcuts (wave 28)

> Superseded in wave 29 by [drawerRevert.md](drawerRevert.md): the code described here is removed and the pre-wave-26 wheel disc is back.

Source: the owner's screenshot of the expanded app drawer (bottom-left corner of a Glance home) and text: the drawer, when expanded, is a looped alphabet wheel; up to four shortcuts can be put in there, or none; the shortcuts are either always open (tapping the four squares opens the wheel) or open only with the wheel; with shortcuts off, the four squares just open the wheel. The apps are plain app icons, not circles (the grey discs in the screenshot are placeholders).

## Decisions
- **Alphabet wheel** (`AlphabetRing`, `ArcDrawer.kt`). A white ring round a black hub in the corner (hub 35 dp, ring out to 90 dp, centre 30 dp in from both edges, as measured on the screenshot). Letters A to Z then `#` (digits, symbols and letters outside A-Z; `arcLetterOf` reuses `wheelLetterOf`), looped: `#` sits between Z and A and the ring has no ends. Letters are 8 degrees apart and read along the arc, tops outward; the letter at 45 degrees is selected (dark disc, white bold letter), letters with no apps are greyed. The hub carries the back arrow (closes) and a white wedge of the ring carries the search glyph (opens Radar).
- **Turning.** Dragging along the ring turns it (`AlphabetRing.rotateBy`), a fling decays (`exponentialDecay`) and the ring settles on a letter with a spring; a tap on a letter goes the short way round to it; the scroll wheel steps letters. Reduced motion: no spring and no inertia, it snaps.
- **Keyboard.** Left/Up previous letter, Right/Down next (looped), a typed letter or digit jumps to it, Tab/Shift+Tab walk the icons, Enter launches the focused icon (the first by default), Esc/Back close, / opens Radar. This reads the owner's "arrow keys step letters" literally; the icons are reached with Tab, not the arrows.
- **TalkBack.** The ring is a labelled node "Alphabet wheel" with the custom actions "Next letter" and "Previous letter"; each letter is an item "Letter X, N apps" (tab role, selected state, click shows its apps); app icons carry their label as content description and a collection position in nearest-first rank order.
- **App fan** (`ArcFan`). The apps of the selected letter are the plain launcher icon (`AppIcon`, as the icon cache gives it: its own shape and the user's icon shape setting; no disc, no clip, no ring, no caption), 48 dp, laid out along chains from the wheel at 90 (the left edge), 75, 60, 45, 30, 15 and 0 degrees (the bottom edge). The first icon of a chain is just outside the ring; spacing is 56 dp along the chain, widened on slanted chains (divided by the larger of cos and sin) because square icons touch at the plain pitch; a slot is kept while the whole icon is on screen and clear of every other slot (square clearance) and of the reserved rectangles (the Radar hotspot of the opposite bottom corner and the shortcut row). Slots sort nearest to farthest, so apps fill from the wheel outwards. More apps than slots: dragging across the fan scrolls by whole slots (`stepsFor`). Tap launches, long press opens the app menu, long press then move drags (`appDragSource`).
- **Shortcuts** (`ArcShortcuts`, three prefs in `launcher_prefs`, exported like the others: `drawerShortcutsOn`, `drawerShortcuts` as newline-joined app keys, `drawerShortcutsShown` Always or OnlyWithWheel; no migration, default Off). Ground Control > App drawer: "Shortcuts" Off / On; when On, "Shortcuts are shown" and "Choose shortcuts" (the picker: four slots, move earlier/later, remove, list of apps to add that disappears at four).
  - Off: nothing anywhere; the four squares open the wheel; the two other rows are hidden.
  - On + Always (`ArcShortcutsRow`): the apps sit on the home next to the four-squares glyph, in a row along the bottom edge when it ends before the page indicator zone (half width 56 dp in the middle), otherwise as a column up the side edge above the glyph (portrait phones); lifted above the dock reserve; hidden while any overlay is open. The four squares open the wheel and the shortcuts stay: the wheel also draws them as its bottom row.
  - On + Only with the app wheel: nothing extra at rest; they appear as the wheel's bottom row and leave with it.
  - Without a four-squares corner hotspot (hotspots none or one that does not hold All apps) nothing shows at rest; the wheel stays reachable through the shortcut chord, gestures and the Radar glyph, and still shows its row.
- **Host.** `ArcWheelHost` is what the home puts in the tree (the at-rest row plus the wheel), so the three modes are tested without the whole home.
- **Cheap draw.** One Canvas for scrim, ring, dividers, letters and hub (remembered strokes, remembered text layouts, the opening scale as a draw transform); icons are placed in the layout phase from animated state.

## Removed
The wave 26 switcher (wedges, count labels, section colours), the Concepts colour-picker fan (concentric rings of rotated cells, mode row, ring actions on the hub, per-ring drag), the `switcherStyle` pref (key retired in `RetiredPrefKeys`, purged once, dropped on import), the "Switcher: Compact or Expanded" and "Wheel groups" rows. The grouping helpers (`buildWheelGroups`, the category cache) stay for Radar and the tests; the wheel itself always groups by letter. The puck's ring actions are no longer drawn by the wheel.

## Not done / device-only
- Nothing here was run on a device; the screenshots are Robolectric renders. Fling feel, the 48 dp touch ergonomics on the dense fan and the placement at rest next to real dock and indicator are device-only.
- The user's icon shape setting still shapes the icons (it is the icon cache's own look); with the default shape the icons are rounded squares, never discs.
- The grey placeholder discs and the exact chain angles of the owner's picture are approximated; the chains are the brief's 15-degree steps with greedy de-overlap, so near the corner some chains start further out than others.
