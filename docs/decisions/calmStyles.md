# Wave 55 calmStyles: a handful of non-occluding calm styles, plus Regular

Owner: "I want only a handful calm computing styles to show signal and battery states. Someone can choose to show the regular style of battery and signal as well. I want to see my beautiful live wallpapers in the background so let's not interrupt it." Nothing here was run on a device.

## Decision
- **One Calm style** (`CalmStyle`, pref `calmStyle`; Ground Control > Look > Indicator, drawn cards): Off, **Regular**, and four ambient styles. Kept from the old set by what is cheapest and what can be drawn at the edge: Animated dots became **Edge dots**, Dot bloom became **Corner bloom**; **Bottom glow** (a wallpaper effect of wave 15) and **Edge beam** (the Beam wallpaper's line, as a level meter on the trailing edge) are new accents. Removed: the Alert effect (Coloured vignette, Comic-book lines), the full-screen dot lattice, `CalmEffect`, `AlertEffect`, `IndicatorSelection`, the Radar `alertLayer` slot and their strings.
- **Why these four.** Each is a few dozen to about two hundred circles or two rects, one Canvas, no bitmap and no per-frame allocation beyond `Offset` values; each works at the edge or in a corner, so none needs the middle of the screen; each reads the same severity.
- **Metric.** Stays Signal or Battery (Show). Showing both ambiently as two tones was not simple (one reading drives one colour and one length) and Regular already shows both at once, so "both" is Regular.
- **Non-occluding rule** (`CalmStyles.MAX_ALPHA` 0.35; the intensity slider scales the cap, default 0.6 so 0.21): drawn on top, never a fill, scrim, vignette or tint; the middle 60 percent is never drawn; the middle 80 percent changes by a mean overlay alpha below 0.04; composited layers of one style (glow plus line, track plus beam) stay under the cap.
- **Expression.** Severity is `IndicatorSeverity` unchanged (Battery 60 percent, Signal 75 percent of the scale, urgent offline or Battery at 5 percent not charging, charging healthy, airplane an Idle reading). Healthy = 0.7 of the cap in the ramp colour, rising to 1.0 as the value falls (the ramp moves to amber and red); urgent = full cap, fixed red, 3.2 s pulse (it was 1.2 s). Edge beam length and Bottom glow height follow the value.
- **Calm.** 6 to 7 s cycles. One still frame under reduced motion, in battery saver (`rememberBatterySaver`, the picture playback rule), for the Idle reading and at intensity zero; the clock loop starts only while something moves.
- **Default follows the wallpaper.** `LauncherPrefs.calmStyle` is null until the user picks: `CalmStyles.resolve` gives Edge dots on the Signal wallpaper and Off on Solid, Gradient, System live wallpaper and Picture (stills, GIF, WebP, video). The settings row shows the resolved style as selected. An explicit choice always wins, per surface the style is drawn over that surface's wallpaper.
- **Regular** (`RegularIndicators`, `RegularIndicator` model): four signal bars with a slash when there is no connectivity, a small battery glyph (low colour at 15 percent or less when not charging, a bolt while charging) and its percentage, spoken as "Signal: 3 of 4 bars", "No signal", "Airplane mode", "Battery 87 percent, charging". It sits in `FixedHomeChrome`, so it is on Glance and every page: centred, 16 dp tall, under the music line (at the music line's place when nothing plays, pushed down with the line by the controls notch), so it clears the 64 dp top hotspot squares on any window of 232 dp or wider and does not change where the edge-to-edge music line is. Right-to-left mirrors the order. **Also show regular battery and signal** (`calmRegular`) appears only beside an ambient style.
- **Sources.** HomeScreen now keeps one `IndicatorSource` for Signal and one for Battery and starts each only while a shown style needs it (ambient style on its metric, or Regular). No new permission: signal still comes from `NetworkSignal` (`ConnectivityManager` default-network callback with `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, and the `TelephonyCallback` signal-strength listener, which needs no permission); battery from the sticky `ACTION_BATTERY_CHANGED`. `READ_PHONE_STATE` is not declared and is not needed.

## Migration
`calmStyle` wins when stored. Otherwise the wave 35 pair maps once (`CalmStyles.fromW35`): Bloom to Corner bloom, AnimatedDots to Edge dots; with no calm effect Vignette to Bottom glow and ComicLines to Edge beam; both off to Off. The older `indEffects` set goes through `IndicatorMigration.fromLegacy` to the same. `LauncherPrefsStore` writes `calmStyle` and removes `indCalm`, `indAlert`, `indEffects`, `indInvert` in one edit at start; an import reads the same way. `RetiredPrefKeys.AFTER_MIGRATION` lists `indEffects`, `indCalm`, `indAlert` with proof key `calmStyle`, so a purge that runs first never loses a choice. Theme packs carry no effect fields (checked), so nothing to map there. Search: "vignette", "comic", "alert", "bloom" and "animated" are hidden aliases of the Calm style row.

## Not matched / open
- No device run: how faint the accents look on real wallpapers (a live wallpaper, a video), the real signal and battery readings and the slow cycles are judged from Robolectric stills and pixel assertions only.
- The Signal wallpaper kind is untouched (it stays a wallpaper choice); its own `ComicLines` code in `SignalBackground` is dead (`wantComic = false`) and was left because the wallpaper layering is another stream's; delete it at merge if that stream agrees.
- Calm styles are not drawn over Radar (the home is hidden there); the wave 35 alert over Radar is removed.
- Ambient styles on a very small window draw their corner discs over the corner hotspots at 0.35 alpha at most (the hotspot icons stay above them).
