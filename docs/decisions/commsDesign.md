# Wave 27: Comms design (commsDesign)

Source: the owner's two mock-ups (the Comms screen and the Comms menu) and two follow-ups ("Group by is icons only", "Show list: coloured app icons, miniaturised, not the black-and-white logos"). The mock-ups replace the BlackBerry-Hub styling of waves 25 and 26 wherever they differ. Internal names (module `feature-launchpad`, classes, preference keys, test tags such as `lp.root`) keep their Launchpad names; only user-visible words say Comms.

## Screen
- Header: three-line menu glyph (48 dp target) and the title Comms (28 sp, regular; the name of the app while a Show filter is on). Second row: the clock (system 12 or 24 hour setting, light 40 sp, 12 h shows a small am/pm marker) at the start, four plain glyphs at the end: brightness dial, battery with level (five bars), signal, Wi-Fi. Each is a 48 dp target. Brightness opens the public Display settings (`SystemTileKind.Brightness`, `ACTION_BRIGHTNESS`), battery the battery usage screen, signal and Wi-Fi the internet panel, all through `openSystemPanel`. The clock takes the one hook `onOpenSound: () -> Unit = {}` (header, `LaunchpadContent`, `LaunchpadPanel`); nothing is wired to it here, the merger connects the Sound and Vibration popup.
- Tabs: ALL UNREAD FILES TIMELINE (wave 28 removed USAGE, see usageViz.md; it was ALL UNREAD FILES USAGE TIMELINE), uppercase, letter spaced, 15 sp (a little smaller than the mock-up's scale so five fit a phone), selected in the ink colour, others grey, no underline, scrolls sideways if it must. A hairline under them. The Read history has no tab: it is a row of Ground Control's Comms category (wave 29), and a two-finger swipe or Ctrl+arrow steps over the five tabs (from Read, next is Files, previous is Unread).
- Rows: flat, no stripe, no date band, no group header in the default view. 20 dp margins, 56 dp rounded icon (the app's own launcher icon with a hairline outline so a black or white icon stays visible), 16 dp gap, headline 24 sp, grey second line 16 sp, time 14 sp right-aligned on the second line, inset hairline. Headline and second line: a message shows its text and then the sender, anything else its title and then its text. Row height about 80 dp (the 2x mock-up measures 104 dp pitch; the 80 dp reading was chosen so a phone shows as many rows as the mock-up implies). History rows (Unread, Read) use the same layout; density Compact drops the second line, Expanded lets the headline wrap to three lines.
- History grouping default is now Flat (no bands). Day, App and Sender are chosen with the GROUP BY grid on a history view (conversation maps to sender, app and conversation to app).

## Comms menu
Panel 58 percent of the window (at most 380 dp), #1A1A1A in the dark look; scrim and the list shift right behind it as before, the home card still slides out. Content: GROUP BY label, an icons-only 3 x 2 grid (time, app, sender / conversation, app and conversation, none) with hairline dividers, the selected glyph in the full ink colour and the others dim; names are accessibility descriptions only. "Show": All Comms first with a neutral comms glyph, then one row per app with its real coloured launcher icon at 24 dp (same cache, loaded off the main thread, 8 dp gap, a coloured letter badge only when no icon loads) and the count right-aligned, dim. No Options section (wave 29): those rows live in Ground Control, see commsOptions.md. Choosing a grouping, an app or an action applies it and closes the panel, density keeps it open. The app filter also filters the history tabs.

## Removed (not in the mock-ups)
Floating compose button and the Radar hook in `HomeScreen.kt` (`onCompose`), the four square system buttons row, the jump strip of names, the stripe, the `...` button and the inline hub options panel, the hub chip rows (range, unread only, app and sender chips; the state keeps the filters, the menu's Show rows drive the app filter), the "Home" text button, the panel's own title and close button (the scrim and Back close it).

## Palettes
Dark: page #0F1219, panel #1A1A1A, ink white, second ink #C9CDD6, dim #6E7587, hairline #262B38 (read off the mock-ups). Light: derived, the shared light tokens (page #F3F4F8, panel white, ink #0E1018, dim #666C7C, hairline 12 percent black); the same layout with the same contrast order. The dark tokens used elsewhere are unchanged.
