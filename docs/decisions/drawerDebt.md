# Drawer disc debt (wave 33)

Debt left by waves 30 to 32 on the all-apps disc (`WheelDrawer.kt`, `PuckSections.kt`, `PuckSectionsGeometry.kt`, core-input `Puck.kt`). No new user-visible strings.

## Decisions
- **Typed letters and Next past Z.** The ring is A..Z, '#', A (see [drawerLoop.md](drawerLoop.md)). A typed letter with no group at or after it now lands on the '#' group when there is one (else the first group, as before); the right arrow past the last letter already stepped to '#' (the ring order) and a test now pins both. Category and Recent modes are unchanged.
- **TalkBack section nodes.** Each node is its own slice of the band: 48 dp deep radially (the radial direction is shared with no neighbour), and as wide along the band as fits inside its own sector, measured at the node's inner edge and cut 1 percent short (`WheelGeometry.bandNode`). The node is turned (`graphicsLayer.rotationZ`) to lie along the band, so hit testing follows the slice rather than a 48 dp upright square. The rectangles of neighbours therefore never overlap (separating-axis test at every rotation). The 48 dp minimum holds radially always and along the band wherever the slice allows it (a one-spoke group from a 360 dp window up); on a 320 dp window a one-spoke slice is about 46 dp. The count, order and actions (select, next, previous) of the nodes are unchanged, so swipe navigation is as before.
- **Spoke spacing.** Two upright square icons of one group on the 45 degree diagonal need centres at least icon x sqrt(2) apart; the old spacing of icon + 10 dp overlapped them by up to about 10 dp. `WheelGeometry.spokeArc` is now max(icon + 10 dp, icon x 1.42 + 1 dp), the same rule the radial step already followed from wave 30, and the angle is the chord's (`spokeStepDegrees`). Cost: a group of N spokes is about 17 percent wider for 45 dp icons, so about 15 percent fewer groups are visible at once; the number of apps per spoke does not change. A fixed spacing is used on purpose: a spacing that varied with the rotation would make the layout depend on it. Tested at every rotation (0.5 degree steps) on six window sizes in both orientations.
- **Dead code.** `PuckLayout.compute`'s ring slots, `PuckSlot`, `capacity`, `clearance`, `insideWindow`, the Radar-button and action sizes and the `MIN_*`/`MAX_ACTION_COUNT` constants are gone with their four tests. `PuckLayout` is now `hubRadius`, `LABEL_BAND` and `compute(minSideDp)` giving the hub and label radii. Nothing else referenced them (`PuckActions` has its own slot type and fixed four).
- **Fan geometry.** The Back hub is 44 dp on a window of 411 dp or more (it was 33 dp; it scales down with the window to 24 dp). The shortcut ring now continues as a full annulus round the hub, drawn in the same colour behind the four wedges, so the hub and ring read as one disc that only the screen edges cut (the window clips it); the flat ends and the protruding hub are gone. The extension is inert: the four wedges, their dividers and their taps stay inside the quadrant.
  Ring depth: the owner asked for about 56 dp if the wedges still keep 44 dp across the arc at the outer edge. They do not: at 56 dp deep the outer radius is 100 dp and a quarter of a quarter turn is 39 dp there. A 44 dp arc needs an outer radius of at least 112 dp. So the outer edge stays where the labels leave room (119 dp on a 411 dp phone, 46.7 dp across), and the depth is what is left outside the bigger hub: 75 dp instead of 85, 1.7 x the hub radius instead of the reference's 1 x. Wedges are 32 dp across at mid radius (30 before).
