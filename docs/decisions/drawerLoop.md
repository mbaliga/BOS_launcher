# App drawer disc: the sections loop (wave 30)

Source: the owner on build 59: "The categories or alphabet sections must be looped... So if I go to A, I should see # preceding it, and seamlessly scroll to it as well." Applies to the restored wheel disc (drawerRevert, wave 29): letters A..Z and '#', or the categories, depending on the wheel grouping setting.

## Decisions
- **Periodic angle.** The groups lie end to end on a ring of `layout.span` degrees. When the ring is at least as wide as the drawn window (quadrant plus the 30 degree cull margin each side, `WheelGeometry.loops`), the rotation is no longer clamped: it is an unbounded `Animatable` value that means "the same picture" modulo `span`. Dragging, flinging (`animateDecay` crosses the seam with its velocity intact), springs and scrubbing never snap back or bounce. The value is wrapped silently (`wrapRotation`) when an animation ends, only to keep the float small.
- **Cyclic drawing.** `WheelGeometry.visibleSegments` and `visibleSpokes` return `(index, copy)` slots: group `i` at `copy` turns of the ring away. Bands, hairlines, labels and app spokes are drawn for those slots, so at A the band shows `... Y Z # [A] B C ...` and at Z it shows `... X Y [Z] # A B ...`; the focused group always has neighbours on both sides. Spoke icons are keyed by `(app key, copy)`.
- **Small rings.** A ring narrower than the drawn window (few groups: one to four letters or categories, or `Recent`) would show its groups twice, so it is not looped: each group is drawn once, centred and clamped as before (n = 1 unchanged). Navigation by key, notch or TalkBack still cycles through the groups.
- **Focus.** `focusedSegment` takes the wheel angle modulo the span. `rotationToGroup` picks the winding of a target group nearest the current rotation: any jump (a tapped band, a typed letter, Home/End, a step) takes the shortest path, across the seam when that is nearer. The initial rotation centres the first group (A) so '#' is visible before it.
- **Scrub along the band.** The first touch of a scrub maps the finger's place along the arc to a group (absolute, as before) at the nearest winding to the current rotation (no jump); further finger movement then turns the ring one whole ring per quadrant sweep, unclamped, so a scrub can cross the seam and go round. Release settles on the nearest group.
- **Keyboard.** `PuckKeys.step` wraps (Left on the first group goes to the last, Right on the last to the first; one group stays), Home/End jump to the first/last group, a typed letter past the last A-Z label wraps to the first group. The scroll wheel and trackpad notches use the same cyclic step.
- **TalkBack.** The disc has the custom actions "Next section" and "Previous section" (wrapping), and one accessibility node per visible band, "Section X", at least 48 dp, with a selected state and a click that focuses the section. Touch stays handled by the disc itself, so the nodes add no extra hit targets.
- **Band colours.** An odd ring (27 letters) would put two equal shades side by side at the seam; its last band takes a third shade.
- **Unchanged.** The app fan of the selected group, the puck ring actions, the grouping chips, the quick-switcher arc, mirrored corner, RTL order, 48 dp targets, hardware keyboard and the open/close animation. No motion-pref change: there are no new infinite animations.
- **Reverts a wave 29 note.** drawerRevert listed "`PuckKeys.step` clamps at the ends again". It wraps again by the owner's request; `INP_18_left_right_rotate_and_clamp` became `INP_18_left_right_rotate_and_wrap`.
