# App drawer revert: the pre-wave-26 wheel disc is back (wave 29)

Source: the owner on build 58 (screenshot of the alphabet wheel): "The app drawer disc was better three versions back with the shortcuts and everything. Revert to that." Read as: the drawer disc of builds 54 and before, i.e. `WheelDrawer.kt` as it was in commit 98474ec (the parent of the wave 26 arcDrawer merge 09d943a): the puck hub with its grouping chips (A-Z, Category, Recent) and search button, the ring actions (the "shortcuts": Ground Control > Puck actions), section bands, app spokes and the quick-switcher arc. The owner may refine this reading.

## Decisions
- **Restored byte for byte** from 98474ec: `WheelDrawer.kt` (all geometry, `WheelGeometry`, `buildWheelGroups`, `PuckAction`, the keyboard contract) and its geometry test `WheelGeometryTest.kt`. Only two lines differ: the file uses `rememberLauncherHaptics()` instead of `LocalHapticFeedback` (the one haptics helper, wave 27, pinned by `LauncherHapticsRoutingTest`). The old file has no endless animation, so the infinite-animation policy is not involved (`W4MotionAuditTest` counts WheelDrawer.kt as 2 animations again).
- **Removed** (waves 26 and 28): `ArcDrawer.kt`, `ArcDrawerMath.kt`, `ArcShortcutsRow.kt`, `ArcShortcutsPicker.kt` (with `ArcWheelHost`, `AlphabetRing`, the app fan), `ArcShortcuts.kt` in core-data, their tests, `L10nW28arcShortcuts.kt` and its references in the two `L10n.kt` expressions, the three settings rows (Shortcuts, Shortcuts are shown, Choose shortcuts).
- **Prefs.** `drawerShortcutsOn`, `drawerShortcuts`, `drawerShortcutsShown` (and the earlier `switcherStyle`) are removed from `LauncherPrefs`/`LauncherStores` and added to `RetiredPrefKeys` (purged once from `launcher_prefs`; the marker file is now `retired_pref_keys_w29`, so installs that already ran the wave 28 purge are purged again; dropped on settings import, so backups from builds 55 to 58 import cleanly). `wheelGrouping` and its "Wheel groups" row (Ground Control, App drawer) are back.
- **Wiring.** `HomeScreen` shows `WheelDrawer` again with `PuckActionsStore` actions. The open routes were never changed by the arc waves: `openAllApps(anchor)` (apps hotspot on Glance and on pages, Radar glyph, gesture, `OpenDrawer`/`OpenPuck` chords) sets `wheelAnchor` and `wheelOpen`, now rendered by the restored disc; the Glance hotspot roles (right to left swaps them) are untouched. Drawer style "Drawer" (the list space) is untouched.
- **Kept although they touched these files** (later, unrelated): the translated details line and relative times in `AppDrawerSpace.kt` (audit W25), the Comms rename (`LauncherCommand.OpenLaunchpad` label, `CornerActionId.OpenLaunchpad`), `rememberLauncherHaptics` in the drawer space, the HomeOrder / HomePages model. The old `HomeLanding` code that 98474ec still had stays deleted.
- **Reverted with the disc:** `PuckKeys.step` (core-input) clamps at the ends again instead of looping the sections (the loop was the arc drawer's), with its test `INP_18_left_right_rotate_and_clamp`.
- **Tests.** Old disc tests that apply are restored (`WheelGeometryTest`, call signatures in `ScreenshotTest`, `ReducedMotionUiTest`, `InputTouchPathsUiTest`, `INP_18`); new `DrawerRevertW29Test` (apps hotspot opens the disc on Glance, on a page, in right to left order; Back closes; a ring action runs) and `WheelDiscW29ScreenshotTest` (portrait/landscape, left/right corner).
- **Review fix (touch areas).** The three invisible chip touch boxes over A-Z, Category and Recent were a fixed 72 x 40 dp, so on a small window (hub radius about 106 dp) neighbours overlapped and the last drawn one stole the taps of the one beside it. `WheelGeometry.chipTouchBoxes` now cuts each box to the gap to its neighbour, so no two touch areas of the hub overlap. Guarded by `WheelDiscTouchAreaW29Test` (portrait and landscape, every corner, font scale 2.0, nine ring actions).
