# Drawer ring: a big Back hub and a rotatable ring of 6 to 8 segments (wave 38)

Source: the owner: "The black circle that holds the back button of the radial app drawer should be bigger so it touches the edge or is close to touching the edge. The white donut that surrounds it should be segmented across its whole length, and be rotatable as well. Make the shortcuts on it editable from an app drawer section in settings. it can have 6-8 segments." And later: "The app drawer will be the disc type only. There won't be a drawer. Remove that option." Supersedes the geometry of [puckSections.md](puckSections.md) and [puckLook.md](puckLook.md) (colours, states and Back semantics are unchanged).

## Decisions
- **Hub.** The disc's centre sits 63 dp inside the window (`WheelGeometry.insetPx`). The hub radius is now 63 minus 4 dp = 59 dp (`PuckSectionsGeometry.ring`), at least 44 dp, so it comes within 4 dp of the nearest edge. It is whole inside the window, so the arrow is centred on the hub (`hubArrowOffset` centres it on the visible part if a window ever cuts it). Back semantics are unchanged (closes like system Back, traversal index 0). `PuckLayout.hubRadius` has a floor (`MIN_HUB`, 120 dp) so the labels stay clear of hub plus a 40 dp ring on small windows.
- **Ring.** A full 360 degree annulus from the hub edge to just inside the group labels (119 dp on a 411 dp phone, so 60 dp deep), cut by N hairline dividers (N = 6, 7, 8; default 8), clipped only by the window. Position `p` is in segment units: at rest a segment sits centred on the quadrant's diagonal (rest position 1: slot 0 on the thumb's side, slot 1 centred, slot 2 beyond). Slot order and the mirror are as in wave 30. About 3 segments are whole and 2 partly cut show at once at 8 on a phone.
- **Visible and tappable.** A segment is visible when at least 10 dp of it shows at the mid radius; hidden ones are not in the semantics tree and a touch on the rim of one by the hub (a few dp) is no target. Its tap area is the whole visible part. Its glyph is NOT moved onto the part that shows (wave 43 replaced that, see [ringGlyphs.md](ringGlyphs.md)): it stays on the segment's own centre at one fixed size and is drawn only when its whole box is inside the window.
- **Tap size, stated.** At 8 segments a whole segment is 46 dp across the arc at the hub edge, 70 dp at mid radius, 93 dp at the outer edge (6 and 7 are wider) and 60 dp deep: at least 44 dp everywhere. A segment the screen edge cuts has less (its visible arc at mid radius can be as small as 10 dp); the three centred at rest are whole.
- **Rotation.** One circular overlay (hit testing follows its circular clip) owns every touch that starts on the hub or ring: tap runs the segment (or Back on the hub), long press names it (an empty one opens the editor), a drag turns the ring about the centre, following the finger from the touch-down. On release it snaps to a whole segment position; a fling is projected 0.18 s ahead, at most 3 segments, then a spring settles it. Reduced motion: snap to the nearest, no inertia. One haptic tick per segment passing the middle. Touches outside the ring radius belong to the outer wheel unchanged (the wheel's drag handler returns for touches that start inside it). Keyboard: Shift+Left/Right always, plain Left/Right while a ring item has keyboard focus (otherwise they still turn the group labels). TalkBack: custom actions "Rotate ring next" and "Rotate ring previous" on every segment and the hub. The ring state is a plain snapshot float read in draw and layout, so turning never recomposes.
- **Settings.** Ground Control > App drawer (the existing category) now leads with Ring segments (6, 7, 8), Drawer shortcuts (one row per segment, each opening Empty, App... or the action list, plus Reset to default) and Show labels under glyphs (default off; a 9 sp label under each glyph on the visible segments). The size and the toggle live in the `puck_actions` file next to the slots (`segments`, `labels`), which is already exported and classified.
- **Slots.** The store always keeps 8 slots; the first N are the ring, so 8 to 6 and back loses nothing. Defaults: flashlight, alarm clock, notifications bell, Do not disturb, Radar, camera, calculator, system settings (all existing actions with existing launch helpers). A four-slot save (wave 30) keeps its four and gets the defaults it does not already have in the other four, rewritten once (idempotent). The wave 29 `actions` key still migrates through the same path. Wi-Fi, sound sheet and recents shortcuts were not added (they are choosable as actions only where `CornerActionId` has them: Recents yes, the sheets no).
- **The app drawer is always the disc.** Removed: the flat app drawer (`AppDrawerSpace`, grid, list, details, scrub rail), the `DrawerStyle` choice ("All apps opens as"), Drawer view and its preview, Sort by, Quick scrub side, Show the A-Z letters, View switcher shows, and the prefs behind them (`drawerStyle`, `drawerView`, `drawerSort`, `drawerRail`, `drawerRailVisible`, `drawerViewSwitch`, retired in `RetiredPrefKeys` and dropped on import; the purge marker is now `retired_pref_keys_w38`). Every opener (the All apps corner action, the Open drawer command, a restored "drawer" surface, Home) now opens the disc. The disc's own settings (Wheel groups, shortcuts) stay. Tests of the removed drawer (`DrawerOrderingTest`, `DrawerDetailsW26Test`, the drawer parts of `InputTouchPathsUiTest`, `InputW3UiTest`, `ScreenshotTest`) are deleted.

## Not matched / device-only
The reference ring continues unclipped round the hub; ours is cut by the window as asked. The feel of the drag, the fling, the hand-over between ring and outer wheel and TalkBack explore-by-touch need a phone.
