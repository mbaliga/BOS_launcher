# Glance fixed chrome (wave 38, stream w38-glanceFixed): decisions

Owner feedback (verbatim): "1. the four hotspots are supposed to remain constant and fixed as I scroll from the calm home to the page(s) on the right ... 2. The text size of the time should have been the same as the music text ... also, if music is showing there, let it continue to show there right? Remove the time and weather altogether - so the layout becomes the four hotspots, the music at the top, and the dots indicators at the bottom, and the content of the calm glance etc move while they remain constant." Later: the Suggested app (thunderbolt) slot is not wanted.

## What changed

- **Fixed chrome layer** (`FixedHomeChrome`, feature-grid): the four hotspots and the music line (with the pop-in controls notch) are drawn once, ABOVE the home pager in `HomeScreen`, inside the same card box (so Comms, Recents and the other overlays cover and move it exactly as before). It fills the window and has no pager offset, so it stays at the same screen position on Glance, during a swipe and on every page and in edit mode. The page dots were already one fixed row above the pager and are unchanged.
- **Hotspots use the chosen corner style.** The fixed layer draws the existing corner layer (`CornerLayer`, `Corners()` in `HomeScreen`) with the `cornerStyle` pref (Holo, Sony quarter discs, Bubbles), filtered to the corners the Hotspots chips enable, so the style applies on Glance and every page and changes live (`GFX_38_theChosenCornerStyleAppliesToTheHotspotsOnEveryPageLive`). Palette fans, widget peeks, complications and the corner editor (long press) therefore work on every page too; the older Glance-only glyph layer (`MinimalHotspotLayer`) is no longer used by the home (kept for its tests).
- **One hotspot set everywhere (superseded in detail by the item above).** The page `CornerLayer` (the old per-page corners with palette fans, widget slots and complications) is no longer drawn on the pages: the Glance hotspot layer (`MinimalHotspotLayer`, glyph per corner, 64 dp touch squares) is the one hotspot layer and follows the Hotspots chips (Four, Two (bottom), One, None) on every page. A palette slot runs its first item and a widget or complication slot is no hotspot, as on Glance before. A long press on a hotspot opens the corner editor as the page corners' long press did (`onLongPress`). The keyboard and pointer hot-corner paths use the same rule on every page.
- **Music line** shows on every page whenever media is active and the new plain toggle `showMusic` (Settings, Home screen, "Show music"; default on) is on. Same text (14 sp, one line, marquee with faded edges, reduced motion = ellipsis), same notch and controls timeout. A tap on the music line pops the controls in (the old tap on empty Glance surface no longer does: the surface has no tap action of its own, so page taps are never taken). The media listener runs while the toggle is on.
- **Clock in the fixed chrome, on every page** (owner's newest instruction): the clock sits at the top centre of `FixedHomeChrome`, the music line directly below it (clock row 48 dp, then the 40 dp music slot; the notch pushes the title down as before and the clock fades out while the notch is in, because the notch covers the top edge). Default face ANALOG (`ClockStyle.Analog`): 48 dp dial (its touch target too), a thin ring, 12 small ticks, a thin hour hand in the ink and a thin minute hand and centre dot in the accent, no numerals, no seconds hand; DIGITAL: the time in exactly the music text's size (`GlanceText.MUSIC_SP` 14 sp), 12 or 24 hour (`ClockFormat`; System, the default, follows the device). Both have a soft dark halo (the digital one a text shadow) for wallpaper legibility; neither is mirrored in right-to-left. The time comes from `rememberNow()`, the system minute tick (aligned to the minute boundary by the OS), so there is no frame loop and reduced motion needs nothing extra. Tap opens the clock app. No date, no weather, no battery line anywhere.
- **Removed**: the Glance weather ambience layer and its tests, the Glance date, temperature and battery line, "Glance content" (chips in the Home quick sheet and in Ground Control), the Time / Date / Weather / Battery toggles, "Weather effect", and the whole **Clock and status** settings section: clock position, layout, size, day, date, weather, Fahrenheit, the status strip (Time, Battery, Signal, Steps, Next alarm, Ring mode, Storage, Memory, Notifications, Floors, Elevation, Distance, Calories), Health data and Health Connect access. Nothing is drawn at the top of the pages any more (the strip is gone from the pages and the scroll; the clock tile a user places on a page is a separate item and stays). The one clock setting is in Settings, Home screen, next to the Glance rows: Clock (Analog or Digital), and for Digital Clock format (12-hour or 24-hour). The Home quick sheet has no clock row (no natural place; it stays small).
- **Weather network fetch removed**: with the strip gone nothing used it, so `Weather.kt`, the `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` permissions, the location prompt and the Weather settings are deleted (docs/decisions/NFR-16.md updated; the privacy audits now assert no network class and no INTERNET permission). Health Connect and activity-recognition permissions stay declared with `Health.kt` but nothing in the app requests or reads them any more (follow-up: remove them with their audit pins).
- The page editor's Glance tab lost its "Content" toggles for one "Show music" row.
- **Home quick sheet** now reads Pages, Order, Hotspots, (this page's tools), Wallpaper, Ground Control. The Hotspots chips are no longer hidden when Glance is off (the hotspots are on every page).
- **Suggested app removed** entirely: the slot drawing, `Hit.Suggested`, `AppSuggester`, the `suggestion` parameter of `GridHome`, the pref `showSuggestion` and the "Suggested app" toggle. `LaunchEvent` and `LaunchHistoryStore` stay (history).

## Persisted state

- Clock keys: `clockPosition`, `clockLayout`, `clockSize`, `clockDate`, `clockDay`, `clockWeather`, `weatherF`, `strip` and `healthSource` are retired (`ClockRetired`, `RetiredPrefKeys`, dropped on import, deleted on the next save). `clockFormat` is KEPT (a stored 12 or 24 hour choice survives; System follows the device, the locale default). New plain pref `clockStyle` (default Analog).
- `glanceContent`: read on every load as migration input into `showMusic` (a stored `showMusic` wins; otherwise Now playing in the old set, or no old set, means on; an empty old set meant "nothing" so music off). It is deleted on the first save after `showMusic` is written, and `RetiredPrefKeys.AFTER_MIGRATION` keeps it until `showMusic` exists (like `indEffects`).
- `glanceWeather` and `suggestion`: retired (`RetiredPrefKeys.BY_FILE`, `DROPPED_ON_IMPORT`, deleted on the next save). The purge marker is now `retired_pref_keys_w38`, so the grown list runs once more.
- `showMusic` is a plain pref in `launcher_prefs` (exported and backed up like the rest; the export is by reflection, `StateCodecTest` covers it). No schema bump (additive and subtractive plain keys).
- Garbage in any old value starts normally (tolerant decode; tests in `GlanceModelTest` and `UpgradeSmokeTest`).

## Not matched / device only

- Whether a swipe that starts on a hotspot's 64 dp square beats the pager, and the swipe feel, are device checks (Robolectric proves bounds, not touch arbitration).
- A widget-peek corner slot now also draws on Glance (it did not before).
- The Digital clock format row shows 24-hour as selected while the stored value is System and the device is on 12-hour (the row cannot read the device setting); choosing either writes the value.
- The test indicator inside the feature-grid chrome test is not wired to the pager; the app-level test uses the real one.

## Superseded by wave 55 (fixedBackdrop)

The Glance page no longer carries the wallpaper: it is drawn once, fixed, below the pager (`docs/decisions/fixedBackdrop.md`). The statement above that the Glance page is "the wallpaper only" now reads: the Glance page is transparent and holds the clock and the gestures; the wallpaper is the fixed layer behind it.
