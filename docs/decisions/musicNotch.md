# Music notch and title (wave 25, owner item 3)

Owner: the music bar is only at the top, not in the dock; the notch must merge gently into the top edge; the title is pushed down when the controls come in and scrolls as a marquee.

- Media controls and the media title exist only in the Glance notch (HOM-23). The bottom MediaStrip, its quick-sheet switch, the stored `mediaControls` preference, the ChromeElement.MediaStrip rule and the dock rail's media item (and DockContent.media) are removed (the owner's rule: only the proposed option stays). Other homes therefore show no media control; the notification-access chip is shown only on Glance with the music line on. The dock stays optional and unchanged otherwise.
- Shape: one Bezier path. Top edge flush at y = 0 and drawn 3 px past the screen edge (no seam); each upper shoulder is a cubic from the top edge (tangent along the edge) to the vertical side, 28 dp wide by 26 dp high, concave; bottom corners are convex cubics. Same black as the status area. The 1 px outline is a vertical gradient stroke that is fully transparent at the top and over the first part of the shoulders, so no line runs along the top.
- Title: sits 14 dp under the status area with the controls away; while the notch is in it is offset by the notch body (48 dp) with a spring (snap with reduced motion), so it keeps a gap under the notch in every state. One line: centred when it fits; when wider than its slot it scrolls (basicMarquee, edges faded into the surface colour). With reduced motion it does not scroll: beginning and an ellipsis. Overflow is decided by measuring the text, not by layout feedback.
- Limits: the edge fade uses the surface colour, so over a busy weather effect the 14 dp fade can differ slightly from the background. Nothing here is device-verified.
