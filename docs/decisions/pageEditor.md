# Decision: the page editor, pages on the side and tabs with their content (wave 36, owner request)

**Status: built (JVM and Robolectric tested; nothing is device verified).** Affects HOM-01, HOM-12, HOM-04, HOM-05, HOM-23 in `docs/Spec.md`. Supersedes the arrangement of `editCard.md` (card on the start side, carousel on the other, add carousel in a band under the card); the card itself, its shrink transition, drag, drop, resize handles and undo are unchanged.

## What the owner asked
"I want the page editor to look something like this [a light phone-shaped canvas with a narrow left column of text tabs, the selected one large and dark, and tall rounded gradient cards on the right running off the edge, the next one peeking in at the bottom], with the pages shown on the side (in case of pagination) and apps, widgets and other settings appearing on the left (as tabs with content below), or show on the top in case of continuous. When the user taps on the shrunken page, the page should swing into view, still shrunken (in both cases, continuous and paginated)." The mock-up (another app) is a layout idea only.

## What it is now
- **Paginated.** The live page, shrunken to half the window width, sits in the middle of a vertical strip of page cards on the END side (right; left in RTL or with the right-to-left page order, either one mirrors it) with its neighbours peeking above and below; the card is 4 percent off the edge as in the mock-up. The strip is `Glance (when on)`, every page, then an Add page card (a dashed outline with a plus): one tap appends one empty page (`HomePageOps.add` after the last page) and focuses it. The left side holds the tabs (Apps, Widgets, Folders, Settings; the selected title large and full ink, the others small and dim, like the reference) stacked as titles, and their content below them.
- **Continuous.** The same, turned: the strip is a horizontal row across the TOP (live page in the middle, neighbours at its sides; page order mirrored with the right-to-left order) and the tabs are one row BELOW it with the content under them.
- **Landscape.** DECISION: the strip stays on the side in both modes (a top strip would take half of a 360 dp high window); the tabs are a row above their content.
- **Focus card.** The live page stays the home's own layer (one `graphicsLayer`, `EditCardGeometry` frame plus the swing), so it is the one editable surface exactly as before. Cards in the strip are drawn by `PagePreview` from the page model, never screenshots. The focus slot of the strip is an empty outline (the live page covers it); if the strip is scrolled by hand the outline shows which page is open.
- **Rail.** Beside (paginated) or under (continuous) the live card: Move earlier, Move later, Delete page (48 dp targets; Delete page asks when the page holds something and shows the Undo notice, unchanged). The Settings tab also holds Delete page.
- **Tabs.** Apps: filter field, Personal / Work chips, a grid of apps; a tap adds to the page being edited, a long press drags onto ANY visible card (the live card, or a peeking one: it is placed on that page with the usual rules, "Page full. Add a page or remove an item." otherwise, "Added to page N" when it is not the page being edited). Widgets: Choose a widget. Folders: New folder plus the shortcut, link, file and app group entries that used to be the Shortcuts tab. Settings: for a page, "Pages use the same wallpaper as Glance" (the pages share one wallpaper setting, so it is not per page) and Delete page; for the Glance, its content items and its four hotspots.
- **Glance.** When Glance is on it is the first card (not deletable, nothing can be dropped on it). Focusing it moves the live page out of the way and draws a Glance card in the slot; the Settings tab opens with its options. Edit mode still ends only when the pager settles on Glance (unchanged).
- **The swing.** Tapping a card (not the focused one) reads where that card is now relative to the focus slot (`dx, dy` in px), switches the page at once (no pager glide) and starts a 350 ms FastOutSlowIn animation of the live layer from that offset to rest: translation, a tilt (`rotationX` for a card above or below, `rotationY` for one at the side, 42 degrees), a small scale-up, camera distance 14 dp (`PageEditorGeometry.swing`). The strip scrolls the new page into the middle at the same time. The page stays shrunken throughout. Reduced motion: no translation, no tilt, a 160 ms cross-fade of the live page. Nothing runs when idle (one finite `Animatable`, no infinite animation).
- **Continuous has no pager**, so the editor keeps the selected page itself (`contSel`). In Continuous the live card is the whole scroll surface; a tap on a page card makes that page the target of Add / drops / Delete, swings the surface in and (wave 37, `editorDebt.md`) scrolls it to that page's first column.
- Entry and exit are unchanged (long press, Edit home, Ground Control; Done, Back, Esc). The dock still steps aside.

## Where the code is
`PageEditorGeometry.kt` (pure: layout, strip entries and offsets, swing, drop hit test), `PageEditorHost.kt` (strip, rail, Glance card, ghost seam, notice), `PageEditorTabs.kt` (tabs and their content), `EditCardWiring.kt` (selection, swing driver, add, delete, drop), two lines in `HomeScreen.kt` (the state's mode arguments and the drag ghost above the live page). `EditCardHost.kt`, `PageCarousel.kt` and `AddCarousel.kt` stay for their own tests and for Launchpad-style reuse; the app no longer shows them.

## Not matched to the mock-up
(Wave 37 added the hamburger menu.) the cards are not stacked fully edge to edge (wave 37: a 6 dp gap), and the page content in a card is the real icon grid rather than a flat gradient.

## Device only
The swing feel and tilt (Robolectric has no compositor), drag from the app grid onto a card while the strip is scrolled, and gesture conflicts between the strip scroll and the page's own drag.
