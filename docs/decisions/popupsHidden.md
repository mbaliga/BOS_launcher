# Wave 38 popupsHidden: full-width Comms popups and the Hidden apps page

Owner feedback: (3) "I think I hid the whatsapp icon ... now whatsapp won't show up in search as well. I need a way for hidden apps to be unhidden again." (4) "The comms pop-ups are getting strangely cropped at the right side."

## Popups (item 4)
- **Cause**: the Sound and Vibration and Connectivity sheets were children of the Comms panel. The panel sits under the shrunken home card, which is drawn after it and pushed to the end edge, and the panel already insets its content from the card's sliver. The sheet was therefore only as wide as the panel and the card covered its end (switches, slider ends, "Done"). Earlier waves even padded the sheet by the card's return zone, so the sheet was narrower on purpose; the mock-ups are full width over everything.
- **Fix**: `PopupSheetHost` (feature-launchpad). `LaunchpadPanel` / `LaunchpadContent` take an optional `sheetHost`; with one, the panel keeps the state (`SoundSheetState`, `ConnectivitySheetState`) and hands the sheets to the host, which the app draws (`popupSheetHost.Layer()`, one line in `HomeScreen`) as a full-window child of the root box after the card layer, so it spans the whole window with the scrim over the whole screen. Without a host (bare panel, tests) the sheets are drawn inside the panel as before. Back, Escape, drag to dismiss, focus, RTL, light and dark are the `PopupSheet` ones and did not change; the Comms Back handler is registered earlier than the sheet's, so Back still closes the sheet first.
- **Margins**: content margin 32 dp each side (was 28); the Connectivity trailing glyphs (tune, lock) and "Done" are end-aligned so their ink ends at the margin like the switches, as in the mock-up.
- The Home quick sheet is a Material `ModalBottomSheet` (its own window, full width up to its 640 dp cap) and was not affected.

## Hidden apps (item 3)
- **Mechanisms in the code base** (grep of hidden / Hide): (1) `HiddenAppsStore` (the app menu's Hide; home, the drawer, Radar and Recents read it); (2) Comms Hide rules (`RuleAction.Hide`), made by "Hide this app" on a notification (app-wide) or "Hide <sender>" (narrower). There is no separate per-surface set. Only (1) removes an app from Radar and the drawer; a Comms rule only removes its notifications, which is why the owner could still open WhatsApp from Comms.
- **Model** `HiddenAppsModel` (core-data, JVM-tested): a row per app that is in the hidden set or has an app-wide Hide rule, with its sources named; narrower Hide rules appear only in the "Hidden by Comms rules" list; unhiding an app clears the set and every Hide rule that names its package.
- **Page**: its own tab "Hidden apps" in Ground Control right after App drawer (the tab carries the count, "Hidden apps · 2"), holding one row "Hidden apps" (value "2 hidden" or "None") that opens `HiddenAppsPane`: search field, icon + name + source per app, Unhide (one confirmation line first), Unhide all (one line), and the "Hidden by Comms rules" list with Remove. The older Home items dialog row stays (it unhides only the set).
- **Radar**: in the flat results list (Flat layout, and the typing list), when the query matches a hidden app by name, one quiet row at the end "N hidden apps match: Show" expands the matches inline (launch on tap, Unhide button); nothing is mixed into the normal results otherwise. The Orbit and Filmstrip typing layouts have no list, so no row there (see the status note).
- Strings: `L10nW38popupsHidden.kt` (es, fr, de, pt, hi, zh, ja, ar).
