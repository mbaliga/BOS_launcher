# Ring labels on the arc, and the Category tap (wave 44)

Source: the owner: "The A-Z, category and recent texts are slanted but not curved. Please ease curve them. Category is also not getting clicked." Evidence: a screen recording of the disc (bottom-left corner). Builds on [drawerRing.md](drawerRing.md), [drawerLoop.md](drawerLoop.md), [drawerDebt.md](drawerDebt.md), [ringGlyphs.md](ringGlyphs.md).

## Root cause of the Category tap
The three group labels (A-Z, Category, Recent) sit at 13, 45 and 77 degrees on the label radius, just outside the white shortcut ring. Each had an axis-aligned 72 x 40 dp click box, composed BEFORE `PuckSectionsLayer`. The ring's touch overlay is a `Box` of 2 x ring-radius square (clipped to a circle for drawing, but Compose hit-testing found the touch inside the square): on the 45 degree diagonal the square reaches 1.41 x the ring radius (about 168 dp against 119 dp), which covers the whole Category label (126 to 140 dp). Compose gives a touch to the topmost sibling whose bounds contain it and never offers it to siblings below, so the overlay swallowed the tap (and returned without acting because the point is outside the ring). A-Z (283 degrees) and Recent (347 degrees) lie near the axes, outside the square, so they worked. Reproduced in Robolectric by touch position: Category 0 of 5 radii hit, Recent and A-Z hit. The screen recording shows the same: the touch dots on "Category" never change the selection while the Recent tap does.

## Decisions
- **Touch layer above the ring.** One layer (`Modifier.ringLabelTap`, a `PointerInputModifierNode` that shares pointer input with its siblings) is composed after the ring. It answers only when the touch goes down inside a label's slot and comes up inside the same slot within twice the touch slop; everything else (the ring, the spokes, the outer wheel's drag and band tap) is untouched because the node shares and never consumes outside a slot. The old rectangular boxes are gone.
- **Slot = the curved label patch.** `RingLabelGeometry.slots`: the label's text span plus 6 dp each side, at least 48 dp along the arc at the label radius where the neighbours allow, never past the midpoint to a neighbour (so slots never overlap), radially from the ring's edge (label radius minus 7 dp) to the hub's edge (plus 14 dp). Hit test in polar coordinates, so the patch is curved.
- **TalkBack nodes** stay one box per label on the slot's centre (cut so that no two overlap, `chipTouchBoxes`), `Role.Tab`, selected state, click. They are axis-aligned boxes, not curved: a rotated box's bounds overlap its neighbour's (the wave 29 test forbids that), so the curved slot is the touch area and these boxes are what TalkBack explores. Keyboard (Tab, Left/Right, `PuckKeys`) is unchanged.
- **Text on the arc.** Every label of the band is one `ArcLabel`: one string set with `Canvas.drawTextOnPath` on a circular path (whole string shaped, so Arabic and Hebrew keep joining and direction), each glyph at its own angle and tangent to the circle. Applies to the group names and to the section labels on the looped band (letters, #, category names, Recent). Font size (11 sp), weight, colours and selection styling are as before; the group names got more letter spacing (0.06 to 0.14 em) so their arc is longer and its bend visible.
- **Never inverted.** A run set clockwise has its up side outward; it is used on the upper half of the circle. On the lower half (`RingLabelGeometry.flipped`: sin of the angle above 0) the run goes counter-clockwise (up points inward) and its baseline moves to the other side of the ink so the ink stays on the same radius. The rule uses only the screen angle, so both corners read left to right and the mirrored corner mirrors the rotation (tested at every half degree). At the horizontal edges (0 and 180 degrees) the text is vertical and not flipped.
- **Selection.** The selected group name is bold and bright with an accent underline that is itself an arc under the text (outside the ink for flipped runs). Band labels: the focused one is bright as before.
- **Rotation, reduced motion.** A label is drawn at the screen angle the wheel's rotation value gives (`qs + segment centre + turn + rotation`), so it follows the ring; there is no animation of its own, so reduced motion changes nothing in it.
- **No per-frame allocation.** Paints and the four arc paths per label (text and underline, normal and flipped) are built once per layout and density, centred on angle 0 about the origin; drawing is canvas save, translate, rotate, scale (the unfurl), `drawTextOnPath`, restore. A source test pins that `draw` and the chip canvas build no Path, Paint or RectF.

## What is not matched
Curvature is geometry: text 60 dp long on a 126 dp circle bends by about 4 dp over its length (about 8 px). Letter spacing makes it longer, not more curved; a single letter (most band labels) has nothing to bend, it is only rotated tangent. The old chips were already on a path (they looked straight for that reason); the band's letters were straight runs rotated about their middle.

## Device-only
Real fonts (the Robolectric host's fonts stand in for the device's, Arabic and Hebrew shaping included), how the bend reads at finger distance, the tap feel on the diagonal slot, TalkBack exploration over the rotated nodes. Nothing here is device-confirmed.
