# Wave 56: settingsCore (CFG-28 to CFG-33)

> **Wave 57 (settingsTabs) supersedes decision 2's section table keyed by row id and decision 9's "row above the groups": the sections come from the tab placement table and Finish setup sits above the tabs; the page resets are per tab (`docs/decisions/settingsTabs.md`).**

Source: the owner asked "Look at clackpad and emulate the settings (Clackpad config) from there for our settings"; the study is `docs/handoff/clackpad_vs_liftoff_settings.md`. This wave builds its items 2, 3, 4, 10 and 11 (search, sliders, reset, rows that are off, Finish setup) and the report-only half of item 5. The Looks rail (item 1) and the calibrators (item 9) belong to other streams.

## What was ported, what was not
The behaviours were taken; the Clackpad widgets were not. The recorded owner rules still win: flat hairline rows and no boxes (the default tick is a 2 dp mark on the track, "Default" is a text action, Reset is a text row, the undo line is a flat line with a hairline), drawn cards rather than text choices (untouched), pages rather than popups (a dialog only confirms, and it is the existing stacked one: the primary action a coloured box on top, Cancel plain text below), and the floating Preview stays.

## Decisions
1. **The hidden side of a row is one object, `ItemMeta`** (id, keywords, disabled reason, pane entries, snapshot-first flag) on every `SettingItem`, set with small extension calls (`keywords(..)`, `disabledUnless(..)`), not new constructor parameters. The data classes keep their trailing-lambda call syntax, so the many call sites and the other streams' edits do not change. `Slider` is the exception and gets real `default` and `auto` parameters (they are its own data).
2. **Ids, synonyms, slider defaults and units live in one table of the app (`SettingsRowMeta`)**, applied once over the built categories. Reason: the Look block and the corner and gesture rows are being edited by other streams in the same file; a table keyed by the English title keeps this wave out of their lines. A test requires every slider to be in it (default and unit), every section row id to exist, and the pane row ids to match the ids the panes draw with. The weakness is the one the section table had (a rename must update the table); the section table is now keyed by id, so a rename costs one line in one place and a row can carry its id at its construction site when its owner prefers that.
3. **Synonyms are English only.** The titles are translated, the hidden words are not (it would be a second translation table of about 400 words for words nobody sees). A user who types in another language finds rows by their translated titles and hints, as before.
4. **Pane rows are declared, not discovered.** A pane's content is a composable; it cannot be listed. A pane that wants its rows searched declares them (`paneEntries`) and draws them with the same id (`ActionRow(flashId)`). Done for Backup, Access and Open with. Not done: Themes (another stream's file), Drawer shortcuts, Workspaces, the dock panes and the Search providers panes.
5. **A row that is off stays.** The reason replaces the summary line, the row is dimmed to 45 percent, does not react, and TalkBack reads "title, unavailable, reason" with the disabled state. A rail of cards is covered by a layer that takes the touches. The reasons that were already written in the gestures category ("not on <style>") stay as they were. Where a row had two variants (the spacing and margins of a paged and a scrolling home) the variants were kept and only the paged-only rows were turned into dimmed rows; "Spacing" is one row that is dimmed on homes that have no spacing.
6. **Reset is preferences of `LauncherPrefs` only.** The per-page field lists are written out (`SettingsReset.PAGES`) and the exclusions are written out with a reason (`EXCLUDED`: first-run flag, device-bound ids and pictures, the home page index, the clock's placement and the clock tile's options, the Lab signal override). Two tests keep them honest: one calls every row's setter against a recording model and requires the fields that change to be inside the page's list; the other requires every field of `LauncherPrefs` to be on a page or excluded. A runtime derivation was rejected: calling setters to find out what they change would also run the setters that write other stores (Comms options, the ring). The fields are reset by reflection over our own data class (`PrefsReset`), the same technique `StateCodecTest` uses.
7. **What a page reset never touches** (documented in `SettingsReset` and the confirm text): the home layout and pages, hidden apps, corner and gesture assignments (the Corners page resets the style, size and behaviour, not what a corner does; the Gestures page resets the motion settings, not the bindings), Comms groups, rules and options, workspaces, themes, the dock's stores. Those have their own screens. The Side panel page therefore resets the left page, the unread strip and the Comms theme only. Pages, Hidden apps, Workspaces, Backup, Desktop and dock, Capabilities, Lab and About have no page reset.
8. **Undo is a restore of the snapshot taken first**, through the same `StateService` snapshots the Backup page lists (so it also shows there). It restores everything the snapshot holds, so a change made between the reset and Undo is undone too; the line stays 9 seconds and goes at the next reset. If the snapshot cannot be taken, nothing is reset and the line says so.
9. **Finish setup is a category that is not a row of the groups.** It is the last category of the list (so no index before it moves), carries one bare pane, is filtered out of the groups and shown as the one flat row above them. It goes away by not being built; the host closes a page that no longer exists. The Do Not Disturb step has no grant explainer of its own (the explainers are a fixed set of five kinds); it uses the existing Do Not Disturb link. Default home uses the existing role request. Usage access and notification access use `GrantHooks`.
10. **Auto** is used where a preference really follows another: the vertical icon spacing. Glow, icon size and density have no "absent" state in the data, so they are not Auto.

## Not done
Dock rows when the dock is forced (inside the dock panes, another stream's files), the Look block's conditional rows (Backdrop dimming, Also show regular battery and signal) and the gestures' density row (the same reason), pane rows for the Themes, Drawer shortcuts, Workspaces and Search providers panes. The help diet is a report; it fails nothing.
