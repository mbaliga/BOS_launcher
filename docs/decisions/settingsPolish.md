# settingsPolish (wave 58, CFG-65 to CFG-69): help diet, the last rows that are off, pane search, consistent tabs

Source: the Clackpad study's item 5 (`docs/handoff/clackpad_vs_liftoff_settings.md`: "every row's summary is either a state value or at most a few words ... cap prose rows at about 5 % of rows ... put consequence help on the destination page header, not in an (i) popup") and the "Not done" list of wave 56 (`docs/status/w56-settingsCore.md`). The owner wants calm, visual settings. Nothing here is device verified.

> This note supersedes, in `settingsTabs.md` (wave 57), the section header names of the Home, Look, Apps, Side panel and System tabs (the list is below and in Spec CFG-69), and in `settingsCore.md` (wave 56) "The help diet is a report; it fails nothing" and the "Not done" items it lists for dock rows, the Look block rows, the Gestures density row and the Themes, Drawer shortcuts and Search providers panes (Workspaces is Profiles since wave 57).

## Decisions
1. **The grey line is a state or at most six words.** `SettingsHelpDiet` (app) defines the line exactly as the row renderers draw it (the reason while a row is off; Action rows draw value and hint joined by " · ") and counts words that contain a letter or digit, so "3 × 6 · 5 × 2" is a state, not prose. Six, not the eight of the wave 56 report: the owner's examples are one to five words, and six keeps "Sends what you type to Google". The guard walks the real categories in six configurations times two page layouts (every conditional row and hint variant appears in at least one). The allow-list is empty; it exists (with a 5 percent cap tested) so a later row that truly needs a longer line says why.
2. **Where the long help went**, per row, in this order of preference: (a) deleted when the drawn cards already show it (home Style on the Pages home, Pages, Corner style, Clock style, Layout, Left page, Comms theme, Right side, Radar background, Wheel groups, Settings backdrop, Calm style, Widgets position, Launcher vibration, Page turning); (b) cut to a few words that keep the fact that matters (privacy: "Sends what you type to <engine>", "Gets only what you send"; consequences: "Its items stay in the drawer", "The history stays", "Notifications in the shade stay", "Needs usage access", "Needs notification access"; how to reach Settings from a home without empty space: "Hold the clock band for Settings" on PSP, "Long-press the margin for Settings" on List, "Long-press empty space for Settings" on DSi); (c) a section note (decision 3) for the facts that belong to a group of rows. The confirm texts of the destructive actions (Mark all as read, Clear all, Delete history, Reset) were not touched: they are where the full consequence is read before acting.
3. **Section notes, not page headers, for rows on a tab.** Most rows are edited on the tab itself; they have no page of their own whose header could hold help. So a section may carry one short sentence under its grey header (`SettingsTabLayout.Section(note = ...)` → `SettingCategory.notes` → `OneUiSectionNote`). Six notes: Glance, Theme, Calm styles, Web, Comms, Sound and haptics. A test holds every note to one sentence of at most twelve words. Panes keep their own header lines (`pageSubtitle`, and the text at the top of the pane), which were already there and were not shortened.
4. **TalkBack keeps the name, value and state.** Switches still read their title and on/off; rails their title and picked value; a row that is off reads "name, Unavailable, reason" (CFG-31). The shortened lines are what the rows' semantics read as before.
5. **Rows that are off (CFG-66)** use the CFG-31 mechanism: Backdrop dimming (Pitch black), Also show regular battery and signal (Calm style is Off / Regular), Pinch changes grid density (No grid density on <style>; its binding logic is untouched). The dock options are drawn by feature-dock outside the settings rows, so the mechanism got two shared pieces in feature-grid: `OneUiSwitchRow(disabledReason)` and `OneUiDisabledBlock(reason, label)` (dims a block of drawn cards, takes its touches, one screen-reader node "label, Unavailable, reason"). With the dock off, the style, magnify, position and mode cards and the group chip and Desktop item switches are dimmed ("Dock is off"); Show the dock never is (it is the way back). Where a surface forces the dock (the desktop, Glance with Right side = Dock rail) Show the dock is no longer hidden: it stays, on, dimmed with why. The Settings Dock page itself cannot tell that Glance forces the dock (it has no home state); only the dock's sheet passes the reason.
6. **Pane search entries (CFG-67)** follow CFG-28: declared, not discovered. Themes, Search folders and Search history are in `SettingsRowMeta`'s pane table; Drawer shortcuts declares one entry per segment the ring has (so "segment 8" finds nothing on a 7-segment ring) plus Reset; Profiles declares its five rows (`ProfilePaneIds` in feature-dock, so the page and the index share the ids); Search providers declares one entry per app that offers a provider, read when Settings opens (`SettingsModel.searchProviders`, the same discovery the pane runs). A pane table entry now adds to entries a row already declared instead of replacing them. A lit row also carries a `PaneFlashed` semantics value (the hit's id), so a test can find exactly which row lit.
7. **Consistency (CFG-69).** Headers that repeated a row of their own section were renamed or merged: Pages → Home pages; Dock + External displays → Dock and displays; Looks + Theme → Theme; Font + Icons → Font and icons; Profiles + App groups and sessions → Profiles and groups; Hidden apps + Open with → Hidden and default apps; Groups and rules → Rules and gestures; Right side → Right edge; Access and privacy → Permissions. Row titles were not renamed (search, deep links and other streams' tests name them). Checked by test on every tab: sentence case, unique headers, no header equal to a row title of its section, no "Other" orphans, a page reset on every tab, an on/off setting is a switch and a choice of 2 to 9 is drawn cards.
8. **Strings.** New and changed strings are in `L10nW58settingsPolish.kt` (109, eight languages). The replaced English strings stay in the older tables (the translation tests do not require unused keys to go), except two of the wave 28 table, whose audit requires every one of its strings to be used.

## Not done
- "Grouping & rules" (a pane) and "Groups and rules" (an action that opens Comms' manager) are two rows with near-identical names in Side panel > Rules and gestures; renaming either is a product wording change left to the owner.
- The Settings Dock page shows Show the dock as a normal switch even while Glance with Right side = Dock rail forces the dock (decision 5).
- Unused older strings were not deleted, except the two the wave 28 audit required (decision 8).
