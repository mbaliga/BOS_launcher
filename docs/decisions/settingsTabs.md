Owner: "Keep our style of tabs however, so that the 6 sections are navigable, clear and consistent." (after the Clackpad study, `docs/handoff/clackpad_vs_liftoff_settings.md`, and the recommended IA of `docs/handoff/product_half.md` section 4). Nothing here is device verified.

In `settingsFont.md` (wave 52): the "Pages" root row with its summary, the "Side panel" and "Workspaces" root rows (both survive as a tab and as the first row of Apps). In `settingsCore.md` (wave 56): the section table keyed by row id (`OneUiSections.TABLE`) and the Finish setup row "above the groups" (it is above the tabs now). In `dockSheet.md` (wave 55): "Settings > Desktop & dock > Dock" is now Settings > Home > Dock (Desktop & dock is gone as a page; App groups moved to Apps). The policy "keep only the owner's newest option" applies: there is no second way in.

## What it is
- **Settings home**: the large centred capital title (as before), the search field, the Finish setup row while something is unfinished, then the **tab row**: HOME, LOOK, APPS, RADAR, SIDE PANEL, SYSTEM, then the selected section's rows, flat with hairlines, grey section headers and drawn cards (the existing rows). A sideways swipe (a `HorizontalPager`) moves to the next section and the accent line under the tabs slides with it; a tap on a tab moves there too.
- **The tab style** (`LiftoffTabRow`, `SettingsTabRow.kt`) is the Comms and page editor style: upper case (the locale's own rules, `titleCaps`), letter-spaced (1.2 sp), 14 sp, the selected one in full ink with a 2 dp accent line under it, the others dim, the neighbour brightening as a swipe reaches it; 48 dp targets. The labels spread over the width; when they do not fit (font scale, a long translation) the row scrolls and keeps the selected tab in view. The page editor's own tab row was not changed (other stream's file, its labels are not capitals); the Settings tabs follow the Comms row's capitals and the page editor's accent line.
- **Collapsing**: the title band, search field and Finish setup row scroll away with an upward drag of a section's list (a nested-scroll connection); the bar's small "SETTINGS" title fades in and the tab row stays pinned under the bar. The list's viewport is always exactly the visible part (its height follows the header), so a screen reader's scroll and a test's scroll-to always bring a row into sight. A scroll by the list's own accessibility actions does not collapse the header.
- **Depth**: tab, then row. Every switch, slider, stepper and card choice is on the tab itself (one or two taps); a pane or a long list opens one page deep (two taps); only editors (corner editor, gesture binding, the dock's Edit dock, the per-pane pickers) go deeper.
- **Remembered**: the section shown last, by its English key, in `liftoff_misc` (`settingsTab`, outside the backup: a convenience, not a setting; `PrefsTabMemory`). The Home sheet's Settings row and Ctrl+, open it.
- **Deep links** (`settingsTarget`): "Home settings" (corner, gesture, the home's long press, the app menu) opens Home on the Glance switch; a long press on a page opens Home on that page's Layout row (the page list above it); the disc's empty ring sections open Apps on Drawer shortcuts; the Home sheet's Wallpaper row opens Look on Glance wallpaper; a Radar settings result opens its tab on its row. The row scrolls into view and flashes for 2.2 s.
- **Landscape two-pane** (600 dp and wider than tall): the start pane has the compact title, search, Finish setup and the six labels as a vertical list in the same style (the accent mark beside the selected one); the end pane is the section, under its own large title (so the floating preview keeps its empty band), swipeable like portrait; a row's page opens in the end pane.
- **Right to left** mirrors the tab order and the swipe. **Keys**: Ctrl+Tab / Ctrl+Shift+Tab and the controller's R1 / L1 move to the next / previous section (wrapping); Esc and Back close a row's page, then a query, then Settings. Focus order is the search field, the tabs, then the rows. **TalkBack**: the row is a collection of one row of six; each tab has role Tab, its selected state and its place (so TalkBack says "Tab, 2 of 6"), and its plain title (not the capitals).
- **Search** works over every tab; a hit clears the query, switches to the hit's tab, scrolls to and flashes the row (a hit inside a pane opens the pane and flashes the row there). A result now says its tab and section ("Home › Corners › Holo").

## Where every row lives (one table, `SettingsTabLayout`)
The rows are still built by topic (the Home, Corners, Theme ... blocks of `buildSettingsCategories`, untouched where other streams edit them); one table places each row, by its topic and its English title, into one section of one tab, and the same table names the section headers. A row no table entry takes is an orphan: it still shows at the end of System under "Other" and `CFG_40_everyBuiltRowLandsInExactlyOneTab` fails until it has a place. Placed rows without an id get "topic/title" as their id.
- HOME: Glance (the Glance switch, the clock rows, music, hotspots and the four hotspot switches, controls timeout and its Try it, surface bar) · Pages (Pages mode, Order, the page list and the per-page rows, Add page, Edit home, Widgets folders and icons, Restore previous pages, Page turning, Home panels) · Layout (style, scroll direction, grid follow and density, default density, icon size, spacing, margins, labels, badges, Hide status bar) · Corners (style, icon size, icon type, rotation, landscape set, the four corners) · Gestures and motion · Keyboard and pointer (Always-on keyboard, Keyboard shortcuts, hot corners with a mouse, delay and its Try it) · Dock (the Dock page: live preview and the dock sheet's options body) · External displays.
- LOOK: Looks rail first · Theme (light/dark, accent, themes) · Font · Icons · Wallpaper (Glance and Pages wallpapers moved here from Home screen) · Calm styles (calm style, regular battery and signal, show, glow) · Settings screen and text (backdrop, dimming, wallpaper palette density and text size).
- APPS: Profiles (the Workspaces page, until the profiles stream gives it its Profiles page) · App groups and sessions · App drawer (ring segments, drawer shortcuts, labels, wheel groups, apps kept on pages) · Hidden apps · Open with. There is no Work profile pause row to mirror (it lives on the disc only), so none was added.
- RADAR: Layout · Appearance (background, keep moving) · Input (keep the layout with a keyboard; the two keyboard-with-Radar rows moved here from Home screen) · Sources (web results, actions, files, deep results, contacts, folders, providers) · Web (engine, suggestions; each hint says what is sent: the engine only gets what was typed on Enter or the Search row, suggestions send as you type) · Privacy (history and aliases).
- SIDE PANEL: Left panel (left page, widget) · Panel theme and size · Sections (widgets section and position) · Comms (default view, pinned, clear from the shade, unread strip, read history) · Groups and rules (manager, grouping and rules, Comms gestures) · Right side (right side, recent apps shown, apps opened elsewhere: the right edge is the other side panel) · Clean up.
- SYSTEM: General (language, Restore open screens) · Sound and haptics (interface sounds, volume, **Launcher vibration**: the same `LauncherHapticSettings` store and values as the Comms Sound and vibration sheet) · Access and privacy (**one page listing every grant**: notification, usage, contacts, folders, microphone through the existing explainers, then Do Not Disturb and the default home with their states; plus Android settings) · Backup and restore (export, import and restore; **Open Liftoff Studio**; Reset all settings) · Help and recovery (Capabilities as one page, Crash reports) · Lab (debug or alpha builds only) · About.

Decisions inside the table: the keyboard-with-Radar rows are under Radar (the owner's Radar list says "move here", which wins over the Home list's mention); "Access Liftoff asks for" (the Comms page) was merged into System > Access and privacy so the grants have one place; Capabilities, once a 30-row category, is one row whose page lists them (each still a search entry); Lab and About rows are inline under their headers. Counts in the fullest state (paged home, three pages, Lab visible): Home 87, Look 21, Apps 9, Radar 16, Side panel 20, System 17 (`CFG_40_theRowCountsPerTab`). Home is long; it is cut into eight sections and every row is one swipe of the list away.

## New rows
- CFG-44 **Page** (Home > Pages, paged homes with two or more pages): drawn cards, one per page (its number and the page dots), picking which page the rows below it edit (`SettingsModel.selectPage`; the long press on a page still opens Settings on that page).
- CFG-43 **Launcher vibration** (System > Sound and haptics): Off, Light, Medium, Strong as drawn bars.
- CFG-43 **Open Liftoff Studio** (System > Backup and restore): a page that explains the browser editor, shows its address and opens it in the browser. Studio is not published, so the address is a placeholder on the reserved `example.com` host and the page says so (`LiftoffStudio.PUBLISHED = false`); replace `LiftoffStudio.URL` when it is published. **Import a Studio file…** in Export, import and restore is the same import as Import setup (Studio writes the app's own settings and setup files, CFG-34), named for it.
- CFG-42 **Access and privacy** (System).

## Ground Control
"Ground Control" is "Settings" in the UI: the Home sheet row (`HomeSheetAction.GroundControl` reads "Settings"), the shortcut command ("Open Settings") and the hint toasts ("Turn it on in Settings, Side panel, Right side."). "ground control" stays a hidden search word of the Settings action in Radar (`ActionHits`). Code names (`GroundControl`, `quick.groundcontrol`) were left as they are.

## Removed
The root list of categories (`OneUiRootPage`, `OneUiCategoryPage`, `OneUiModel.GROUPS`, `groups`, `summary`, `fallbackSummary`, the per-category accents, `displayTitle`, `OneUiModel.Nav`), the summary lines (`SettingsOneUiSummaries.kt`: `settingsSummaries`, `sidePanelSummary`) and the `summaries` parameter of `SettingsSpace`, the `OneUiSections.TABLE` and `find`, the `reorganise` regrouping, the category pages "Pages", "Home screen", "Corners", "Gestures", "App drawer", "Hidden apps", "Workspaces", "Desktop & dock", "System & sound", "Backup", "Capabilities", "Lab", "About" as pages of their own, the test tags `oneui.category` and `oneui.row.*`.

## Not done
