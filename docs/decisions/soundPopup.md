# Wave 27 soundPopup: the Sound and Vibration sheet

Owner mock-up: a pure black bottom sheet with a large top radius over the dimmed Comms screen, centred title, four rows (Music, Video and Games; Notifications; Alarms; a hairline; Vibration), each a glyph and label above a thin white hairline slider with a small white rectangular thumb.

## Decisions
- **Where**: `feature-launchpad` (`SoundVibrationSheet.kt`, `SoundVibrationState.kt`), next to the system row whose sound button it replaces. `SoundVibrationSheet(onDismiss)` is the entry point. The header stream calls it through `SoundSheetState.show()` (hook `onOpenSound`): `LaunchpadContent(soundSheet = ...)`; the system row's sound button calls the same, so it works before the merge. The old "open the system volume panel" action for the Sound tile is gone (the other tiles still open their panels).
- **Custom drawn slider**, not Material: 48 dp touch target, 1 dp hairline (brighter and 1.5 dp where filled), 10 x 22 dp white rectangle thumb. Touch (tap, drag), keyboard (arrows step, Page keys, Home, End; mirrored in RTL) and TalkBack (the row node carries `ProgressBarRangeInfo` with steps, a `stateDescription` such as "53 percent", and a `SetProgress` action; label and second line are merged into that node).
- **State is immutable** (`SoundVibrationState`), changed through `VolumeController` (fake in tests) and `HapticControl`; every change is read back, so the slider shows what the system holds.
- **Volumes**: `AudioManager.getStreamVolume/getStreamMaxVolume/setStreamVolume(..., 0)`: public, no permission, no system panel, no sound.
- **External changes**: a `ContentObserver` on the public `Settings.System.CONTENT_URI` (event driven, unregistered on dispose), a re-read on resume, and a 1.5 s poll only while the sheet is open as a safety net. The `android.media.VOLUME_CHANGED_ACTION` broadcast is not part of the public SDK, so it is not used.
- **Do Not Disturb**: the Notifications row is disabled with the reason "Do Not Disturb is on" when the interruption filter is not "all" and the app has no policy access, and also after a `SecurityException` from `setStreamVolume` (caught, never crashes).
- **Vibration**: the system vibration intensity needs WRITE_SETTINGS, which we do not add. The slider is the launcher's own haptic strength (Off, Light, Medium, Strong; default Medium = every earlier build), the row's second line says "Launcher vibration". Stored in pref file `launcher_haptics` (key `strength`) through `TolerantPrefs`, exported with the other launcher settings (`PrefFilesSource.ALLOWED`, classified in `StateSchema` notes and the quality audit). `HapticPolicy` maps strength and kind: Off nothing; Light ticks only; Medium as before; Strong the firmer long-press effect for everything.
- **One helper**: `rememberLauncherHaptics()` (feature-grid) wraps the platform haptics with the strength; every former `LocalHapticFeedback.current` use goes through it (mechanical one-line edits in 11 files) and `MotionGestureSensors` consults `HapticPolicy`. `LauncherHapticsRoutingTest` pins that no other file uses `LocalHapticFeedback`.
- **Dismiss**: Back (BackHandler), Escape, a tap on the dimmed area, a drag down (nested scroll: moves the sheet, closes past 96 dp or on a fling). Reduced motion: fade in instead of the slide. Landscape: the sheet is at most 640 dp wide, spacing tightens and the rest scrolls.
- New strings in `L10nW27soundPopup.kt` (es, fr, de, pt, hi, zh, ja, ar).
