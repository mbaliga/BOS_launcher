# Owner's dot grid (from the owner's SVG, 956 x 440 landscape artboard; the SVG group is rotated 90 degrees so the same design is meant for a 440 x 956 portrait screen too)
- Background: pure black.
- Dots: circles of radius 2 (artboard units) on a 24-unit pitch. Columns at x = 10 + 24*i for i = 0..39 (10 .. 946); rows at y = 16 + 24*j for j = 0..17 (16 .. 424). All dots #D9D9D9 (near white). In the SVG they are a MASK (alpha) so the visible brightness comes from the fill below.
- Fill under the mask: a white rect (x 0..956, y 31..410) at group opacity 0.3 with an ELLIPTICAL RADIAL GRADIENT: centre (478, 220.5), radii rx = 478 (half the width), ry = 189.5 (about 0.43 of the height), white opacity 1 at the centre fading linearly to 0 at the ellipse edge. So the dots are brightest in the middle of the screen at 30 percent white, and fade to nothing toward the edges (top and bottom rows and the far left and right columns are invisible; rows outside y 31..410 are not drawn).
- Owner's addition: "The dots should 'breathe' by glowing and fading." Each dot slowly swells and glows (alpha and a soft halo, radius about 2 to 3 units) and fades back, in smooth waves rather than all at once (for example a slow radial wave from the centre plus per-dot phase offsets, 4 to 7 second period), always staying inside the elliptical falloff envelope so the edges stay dark. Calm, peripheral, never flashing.
