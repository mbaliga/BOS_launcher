## Other findings fixed

- Rule widening: removing a person link (or the last alias) removed the person condition from rules and kept the rest, so a rule "Priya and 9 to 5" became "anyone, 9 to 5". Such rules are now deleted with the person; a negated person condition is just dropped (test `STR_13_removingAPersonDropsTheRulesThatRequiredThemInsteadOfWideningThem`).
- Main-thread disk read: the waiting indicator, composed with the first home frame, initialises the Launchpad rules and groups stores; those two files were missing from `PrefsWarmup` (`NFR_02_everyPreferenceFileTheFirstHomeFrameReadsIsWarmedOffTheMainThread`).
- Translations the coverage test cannot see: ten strings chosen by `if` or `when` inside translator calls (dock visibility choices, the four home style descriptions, "Spacing across", the preview toggle) were English in every language; added for all eight languages in `L10nW13auditCross.kt`. New `W13TranslationDeepScanTest` reads the whole first argument of every translator call; `W13LabelsTest` checks enum labels shown through `L10n.tr(label)` (row commands, landing labels). All strings of the wave 8 to 12 screens were already in the tables, and the right-to-left arrow of the rule summary is mirrored in Arabic.
