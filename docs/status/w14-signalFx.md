# Stream status: Radar background (sweep, signal glow lines, comic no-signal lines, dot grid)

Nothing here is device-confirmed. "built" means it compiles, the pure logic has JVM tests and the drawing has Robolectric render tests (native graphics); the device-only steps are rows in `docs/device-checklist.md` ("Radar background and signal view").

Screenshots from the render tests are written to `feature-grid/build/screenshots/radar-bg-*.png` (dots, one per state in portrait and landscape, dark and light; comic lines; still versus moving; sweep on and off). Two were read and compared with the owner's frames: `radar-bg-good-land-dark.png` against the glow-lines frames (eight grey bracket lines flanking a calm centre, blue pulses on the outer ends, straight runs and the curl toward the middle, never filling a whole line) and `radar-bg-comic-port.png` against the no-signal brief (thin white-grey speed lines from the edges inward, irregular, periphery only, calm middle with the dots). The scope frame (`radar-bg-sweep-on.png`) matches the radar recording in kind (faint rings, crosshair, centre dot, wedge with a bright leading edge and a tail) but is not pixel-compared. Dot grid (`radar-bg-dots-*.png`): brightest in the middle, invisible at the edges, asserted by pixel means.
