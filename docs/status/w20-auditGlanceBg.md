# Wave 20 audit: Glance home, two-slot backgrounds, recents panel, edge handles

Auditor stream w20-auditGlanceBg. Robolectric and JVM tests only; nothing here was run on a device.

## Defects found and fixed

1. Clock clipped (HOM-23). `glance.time` was laid out with `softWrap = false` inside a column padded 76 dp each side; at 360 dp wide, at 320 dp wide with 200 percent font and in landscape on a 568x320 window the text overflowed its own box (`hasVisualOverflow`) and lost digits. Fixed: `GlanceText.clockSizeDp` also caps the size by the room between the two 48 dp handles (`CLOCK_SIDE_CLEAR_DP` 56, `CLOCK_EM_WIDTH` 2.7), the column padding is that same 56 dp, and the text shrinks by a tenth while it still overflows (state write from `onTextLayout`). Tests: `GlanceAuditW20UiTest.HOM_23_theClockIsNotClipped*`, `..._InLandscapeOnASmallWindow...` (also clear of both handles and every hotspot).
2. Right side = Dock rail showed nothing on a phone (HOM-23, STR-14). `DockHost` returns early while the dock setting is off (the default on phones), so the indicator dot "Show the dock", the edge swipe and `openRecents()` did nothing. Fixed: `HomeChromeRules.forcesDock` (Glance and Dock rail) feeds `DockLayer(alwaysShown)` and `DockHost(alwaysShown)`; the rail keeps its place in the focus graph, other landings keep the dock setting. Tests: `GlanceAuditW20Test.HOM_23_theDockRailSettingForcesTheDockOnGlanceOnly`, `GlanceAuditW20WiringTest.HOM_23_theDockRailSettingReachesTheDockHostThroughTheLayer`.
3. Lab forced signal could colour the real background (PAL-24). `radarSignalForce` is a plain exported pref; an import or an upgrade from a Lab build carried it into a build without the Lab and the home and Radar stayed on "No signal" with no way to undo it. Fixed: `SignalForceGate` (feature-grid) ignores the stored force when the Lab is not available, `LiftoffApplication` closes the gate from `LabGate.visible()` and resets the stored value to Live on a background thread in such builds. In Lab builds the force still persists on purpose (the tester leaves the Lab to look at Radar). Tests: `SignalForceGateTest`, `GlanceAuditW20WiringTest.PAL_24_...`.
4. Date, weather and music line were dimmer than the ink the home scrim is sized for (NFR-15). The scrim plan guarantees AA for the theme `InkBright` and `Ink`; Glance drew the date in 9A9AA6 (dark) and 55555F (light), below that guarantee. Fixed: `GlanceInk` uses exactly the theme tokens. Tests: `NFR_15_theGlanceInksAreTheThemeInksTheScrimIsPlannedFor` (guards drift) and `NFR_15_theClockAndTheDateReachAaOverEveryBackgroundAtEveryLevelWithTheHomeScrim` (every item alone, the default pair and three other pairs, dark and light, both intensities, levels 0 to 4 although the app samples 4 and 2, scrim applied).
5. Right-to-left title next to a Latin artist (HOM-23). Each part of the music line is now a bidi isolate for drawing (`GlanceText.nowPlayingDisplay`); the spoken line is unchanged. Test: `HOM_23_theMusicLineIsolatesEachPart...`.
6. Dead rule wired: `GlanceWeather.animates` was never called; the weather layer clock now follows it.
