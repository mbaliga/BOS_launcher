Covers: Glance (wallpaper Solid and Gradient, clock in the five styles at the saved place and size with 12 or 24 hour, date, accent; music line edge to edge when no top hotspot; four hotspots in Holo, Quarter and Bubble with the chosen action glyphs or app icons; the calm styles and Regular; dock Standard, Transparent and 3D, Collapsed or pinned, group chips; surface indicator; theme, accent, icon shape, tint and plate, UI font), Pages (grid, spacing, margins, labels, clock tile, Continuous and Paginated, right-to-left), Radar (Filmstrip, Orbit, Flat, sources, keyboard), the drawer disc (6 to 8 segments with the chosen shortcuts and grouping), Comms (rail, pinned, categories, text size, theme), Recents and the dock rail.
