A home screen that is the surface of a sphere. Your apps sit on a coverflow
equator, magnified continuously at the point nearest you, and the whole thing is
built for landscape first.

It is a real launcher, written from scratch in Kotlin and Compose. Nothing here is
a skin over the stock home screen and nothing requires a modified system.

THE SURFACE
• A coverflow equator with a continuous magnification lens at the front centre,
  not a row of icons with one scaled up.
• Pinch in for the all-apps globe: every app projected onto a sphere you can
  rotate, with the same lens.
• Spread for live surface settings, and tune the geometry while you look at it:
  perspective, spacing, turn, depth, minimum scale, focus, arc and tilt.
• Pull down for the hub.
• One row in landscape, two in portrait, with your focused app preserved across
  the change.

USING IT
• Drag to scrub with a snap, tap to focus and launch, long press for the app menu.
• Type to search, with an always-up keyboard in portrait and live ranked results.
• Hide apps you do not want to see.
• Work-profile aware.

BUILT FOR THE DEVICE YOU HAVE
Hardware-accelerated surface materials are used where the device supports them,
with a plain fallback where it does not. No root, and no system modification.

QUIET BY DESIGN
• No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking.
• Your app list, your hidden apps and your surface settings never leave the device.

This is an early release. The coverflow, the globe, search, the hub and the live
settings all work. Widgets and the full notification feed are still to come.
